04Engineering decisions

01

Keep the boundary explicit

The product is described as community interaction, not clinical care or crisis support.

02

Prioritize low-friction interaction

Anonymous posts and lightweight peer features reduce unnecessary steps in the core flow.

03

Keep the stack inspectable

React, Express, and SQLite keep the project focused on product workflows.

04

Protect account paths

Authentication uses bcrypt as documented by the repository.
